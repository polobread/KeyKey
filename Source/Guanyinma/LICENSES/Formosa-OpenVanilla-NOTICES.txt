Shared Cangjie helpers and generated Unicode data
------------------------------------------------
Source/Shared/CangjiePrefix.h and the common ranking data are original project
material under Source/Shared/LICENSE.txt (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Chui-Ping Cheng).
Guanyinma/src/table.rs retains the shared helper's behavior.

Source/Guanyinma/build.rs converts Source/Shared/CangjieUnicodeData.h into
Rust constants in Cargo OUT_DIR. The canonical CJK mappings and exclusion
ranges derive from Unicode 16.0.0 and retain Unicode License v3.
The complete unchanged Unicode notice is in Unicode-LICENSE.txt next to
this file. Generated constants are data, not a new license grant.
